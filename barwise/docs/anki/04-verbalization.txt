[Produce the FORML sentence] Fact type "Customer places Order" (forward reading "places", inverse "is placed by"). A uniqueness constraint covers the role played by <b>Order</b>. Verbalize the constraint.	Each Order is placed by at most one Customer.<br><br>(Single-role internal uniqueness on a binary: <code>Each {subject} {predicate} at most one {object}</code>, where the subject is the player of the constrained role.)<br><br><i>Read more: Halpin, ORM 2 Constraint Verbalization (ORM2-02); Halpin & Morgan 3rd ed. constraint chapters.</i>
[Produce the FORML sentence] Fact type "Customer places Order". A mandatory-role constraint covers the role played by <b>Customer</b>. Verbalize it.	Each Customer places at least one Order.<br><br>(Binary mandatory: <code>Each {subject} {predicate} at least one {object}</code>, subject = player of the mandatory role.)<br><br><i>Read more: Halpin, ORM 2 Constraint Verbalization (ORM2-02); Halpin & Morgan 3rd ed. constraint chapters.</i>
[Produce the FORML sentence] A value type "Rating" is restricted to the enumeration A, B, C, D, F. Verbalize the value constraint.	The possible values of Rating are: {'A', 'B', 'C', 'D', 'F'}.<br><br>(Enumerated values are single-quoted and comma-joined inside braces.)<br><br><i>Read more: Halpin, ORM 2 Constraint Verbalization (ORM2-02); Halpin & Morgan 3rd ed. constraint chapters.</i>
[Produce the FORML sentence] A value constraint on "Age" allows only values of 18 or greater (a range, min 18 inclusive, no upper bound). Verbalize it.	The possible values of Age are: {at least 18}.<br><br>(A range with only a lower inclusive bound renders as "at least N"; an exclusive lower bound would be "greater than N".)<br><br><i>Read more: Halpin, ORM 2 Constraint Verbalization (ORM2-02); Halpin & Morgan 3rd ed. constraint chapters.</i>

[Produce the FORML sentence] Person authors Book; Person reviews Book. An exclusion constraint covers the "authors" and "reviews" roles played by Person. Verbalize it.	No Person both authored some Book and reviewed some Book.<br><br>(Exclusion: <code>No {player} both {role} some {obj} and {role} some {obj}</code>.)<br><br><i>Read more: Halpin, ORM 2 Constraint Verbalization (ORM2-02); Halpin & Morgan 3rd ed. constraint chapters.</i>
[Produce the FORML sentence] An exclusive-or constraint covers Employee's "is salaried" and "is hourly" roles (exactly one must hold). Verbalize the shape.	Each Employee either is salaried some ... or is hourly some ... but not both.<br><br>(Exclusive-or = disjunctive mandatory + exclusion: <code>Each {player} either ... or ... but not both</code>.)<br><br><i>Read more: Halpin, ORM 2 Constraint Verbalization (ORM2-02); Halpin & Morgan 3rd ed. constraint chapters.</i>
