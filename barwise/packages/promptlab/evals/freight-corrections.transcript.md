Facilitator: Let's map out how shipments move through the network.

Logistics Manager: We track shipments -- consignments, some of the older staff say, same thing. Each shipment has a shipment number.

Facilitator: What goes into a shipment?

Logistics Manager: A shipment contains products, each in a specific quantity. Products are identified by SKU. For example, shipment S-100 contains product P-77 in quantity 5.

Facilitator: Can the same product appear twice in one shipment?

Logistics Manager: No -- for a given shipment and product there is exactly one quantity line.

Facilitator: Where do shipments sit physically?

Logistics Manager: A shipment can be split across warehouses -- up to three warehouses per shipment. Warehouses have a warehouse code.

Facilitator: Up to three?

Logistics Manager: Actually, scratch that. We changed the policy last quarter: a shipment sits in at most two warehouses now. Two, not three.

Facilitator: And at minimum?

Logistics Manager: Every shipment is in at least one warehouse -- otherwise we have lost it.
