Facilitator: Let's talk about how staffing works at the consultancy.

Resourcing Lead: We staff employees onto projects. Every employee is assigned to at least one project, and never more than three at a time.

Facilitator: How are employees and projects identified?

Resourcing Lead: Employees have an employee number. Projects have a project code, and each project also has exactly one project name.

Facilitator: You mentioned mentoring earlier.

Resourcing Lead: Yes -- employees mentor other employees. Each employee has at most one mentor, who is also an employee.

Facilitator: Can someone be their own mentor?

Resourcing Lead: No, never. And you cannot have loops either -- if Alice mentors Bob, then Bob cannot mentor Alice, not even indirectly through a chain. Mentoring always flows one way.

Facilitator: Can one employee mentor several people?

Resourcing Lead: Yes, a mentor can have many mentees. It is the mentee side that is limited to one mentor.

Facilitator: Back to assignments -- can an employee be put on the same project twice?

Resourcing Lead: No. An employee is either on a project or not; there is no double assignment.
