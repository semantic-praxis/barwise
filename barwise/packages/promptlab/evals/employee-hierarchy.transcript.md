Facilitator: Tell me about how people and departments are organized.

HR Director: We track persons generally -- anyone in the system is a person with a person ID. Employees are persons; every employee is also a person, identified the same way.

Facilitator: Are there kinds of employees?

HR Director: Managers. A manager is an employee with extra responsibilities. Every manager is an employee, and through that, a person.

Facilitator: How do employees relate to departments?

HR Director: Every employee works in exactly one department. Departments have many employees, but nobody is in two departments at once, and nobody is unassigned.

Facilitator: What about managers and departments?

HR Director: A manager manages at most one department. Some managers are between assignments and manage none.

Facilitator: How are departments identified?

HR Director: Each department has a department code. Departments also each have a department name, like "Payroll" or "Field Operations", and no two share a name.
